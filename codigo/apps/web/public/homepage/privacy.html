Controller

The four team members named in the imprint, jointly (Art. 4(7) and Art. 26 GDPR). The essence of our arrangement is available on request. Contact: shelfie@gmail.com. No data protection officer has been appointed.

Is providing data required? It is not a legal or contractual requirement. If you decline on the consent screen, the checker app stays read-only and nothing is submitted. You can still read this site.

Location

Your location is read in your browser only when you claim a task and when you take the photo. Our engine's request log does not contain raw coordinates, which an automated test checks. We keep the outcome (distance, accuracy, inside or outside the radius).

Photos and the AI service

Your photo is sent to our server and to an AI vision service, currently Google (Gemini). The photo is downscaled and re-encoded without metadata before it is sent. Google is located in the USA, so this is a transfer to a third country. Google processes the photo under its Gemini API terms. We do not claim that photos are excluded from model improvement.

Photograph shelves and price tags only. Do not photograph people. If a person is visible in a photo, the verifier records it as a note, and that person's image is third-party data; avoid this.

Automated decisions

The verification decision (pass or fail) is made by fixed rules, and it determines whether a test payment is made. There is no human review in this prototype. You may contact us to have a person look at a result. The payment is test money with no monetary value, and we do not take decisions with legal effect on you.

Payout address on the ledger

A payout is a Cardano transaction. It is public on the Cardano preprod ledger and cannot be deleted or changed by us or anyone else, so the right to erasure cannot be fulfilled for on-chain data. Preprod addresses carry no real funds.

Recipients and hosting

  • Vercel hosts the web app and processes technical request data such as IP address and time.
  • Cloudflare provides the tunnel to the engine, which runs on a team machine, and processes technical request data.
  • Google (Gemini) reads the photo for verification.
  • Masumi and Sokosumi process the order and the payment of the task.
  • The Cardano network is public and shows transactions to anyone.

Our engine's request log is designed to store only method, route, status, duration and record ids, not request bodies, query strings or IP addresses. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Cookies and local storage

The current code sets no cookies. The checker app stores your worker id, display name, payout address, consent version and a location hint in your browser's local storage on your device; you can delete it in your browser. Dashboard access codes are kept in memory only. Because nothing is stored for tracking, we show no cookie banner.

Retention

Photos are kept on the team server until the end of the event and then deleted; photos chosen for the pitch are kept only if no person is visible and the author agreed. Payout address and checker records are deleted seven days after the event. On-chain data is permanent.

Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17, except for on-chain data), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You may withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) with effect for the future. Contact: shelfie@gmail.com.

You may complain to the supervisory authority: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach; postal address Postfach 1349, 91504 Ansbach. lda.bayern.de
