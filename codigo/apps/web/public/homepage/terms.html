The service

  1. Shelfie is a prototype for a hackathon. It runs on the Cardano preprod test network.
  2. Payments are test ADA. Test ADA has no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, and nothing in Shelfie promises a payout in real currency.
  3. Taking a check is not employment and creates no employment, freelance or other work relationship with the team or with TUM.
  4. You must be an adult to take a check.

Rules for checkers

  1. Photograph shelves and price tags only. Do not photograph people, staff, customers or anything private. Do not enter staff-only areas, take anything from the store, or move products except as the task describes.
  2. Shelfie has not asked any store for permission to photograph, and we do not promise that photographing in a store is allowed. House rules may restrict it, and in Germany so may the personality rights of people shown. Follow the rules of the store, ask where required, and stop if staff ask you to.
  3. Do not take risks to complete a task.
  4. Use only the live in-app camera and your own location. Do not fake photos, locations or hand signs. We may withhold a payout before it is sent, and we may remove you from the app. A payout that has been sent on chain cannot be reversed.
  5. A test payment is made only if the photo passes the automatic checks. A photo that fails is not paid, even if you acted in good faith.

General

  1. We may change or stop the service at any time. The service is provided "as is".
  2. German law applies. For consumers, mandatory statutory rules apply in any case.
