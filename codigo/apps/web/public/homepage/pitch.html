Proof first, payment second.

  1. Brand posts a job

    The fee waits in escrow on Cardano.

  2. A person photographs

    In the store, with a one-time hand sign.

  3. A verifier checks

    A separate service, paid per check over x402.

  4. Pass or refund

    Pass: test ADA paid, report delivered. Fail: brand refunded.
