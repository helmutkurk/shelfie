It started with a real request

“Can you photograph my competitors in a few shops?”

A friend of Isabel's father wants to bring a drink to Germany. He asked Helmut and Isabel to check comparable products in Munich stores: the price, and whether it is on the shelf at all.

The problem

He can't check our photos. An AI can't see a shelf.

He is abroad. Friends walk for hours, agencies are booked days ahead and bill every visit, whether the photo is usable or not.

The idea

An agent that hires a person to look.

A brand posts a job for one product in one store. Someone nearby claims it and photographs the price tag with the live camera.

The proof

A verifier checks every photo before anyone is paid.

A one-time hand gesture, shown only when the camera opens, proves the photo is fresh. A vision model reads product and price; deterministic rules decide.

The payment

Nobody pays for a photo that isn't true.

The fee waits in Cardano escrow. Photo passes: the person is paid and the report is delivered. No passing photo: the brand gets its money back.

How it works

Proof first, payment second.

  1. 1

    Order

    A brand picks one product and one Munich store from our catalog. The fee is locked in Masumi escrow on Cardano.

  2. 2

    Claim

    The job appears for people in the city. The first one to claim it walks to the store.

  3. 3

    Photograph

    Live camera only, no gallery. A one-time hand gesture next to the price tag proves the photo is new; the location is checked against the store.

  4. 4

    Verify

    A separate verifier agent, paid per check over x402, reads product and price and applies fixed rules: pass or fail.

  5. 5

    Settle

    Pass: the person is paid on-chain and the report is delivered. No passing photo in time: the escrow refunds the brand.
